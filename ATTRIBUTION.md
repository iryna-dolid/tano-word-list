## Words, grades, categories, pronunciation

**English:** Word data adapted from the Basic Korean Dictionary (한국어기초사전) and the NIKL Korean–English Learners' Dictionary by the National Institute of Korean Language (국립국어원), https://krdict.korean.go.kr, used under CC BY-SA 2.0 KR (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/kr/). Changes: English glosses shortened, edited and disambiguated; romanization, frequency ranks, learning order, levels and app categories added; one of the dictionary's learner example sentences selected per word, with an English translation added (AI-drafted, spot-checked); 205 cards with the same word and meaning as another card (a number and its form before a noun, a noun and its adverb, the -적 words…) merged into that card, their example kept as its second one. The adapted word list is available under CC BY-SA 4.0 at https://github.com/iryna-dolid/tano-word-list.

## Example sentences

The Korean example sentences are the Basic Korean Dictionary's own learner examples (한국어기초사전 용례), unchanged. **Their English translations were drafted with AI** (Anthropic's Claude) for this app and spot-checked; they are part of the adapted word list and shared under the same license. Words whose dictionary sense has no learner example show none.

## Level sizes

Level boundaries use the cumulative vocabulary sizes of NIKL's international standard curriculum for Korean (국립국어원, 2018, 국제 통용 한국어 표준 교육과정 적용 연구).
